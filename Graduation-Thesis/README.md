# Graduation Thesis

## Title

Improving Bidding Participation Activities in Construction Consulting Enterprises

## Institution

National Economics University

## Research Objective

This study evaluates bidding participation activities in a construction consulting enterprise and identifies practical solutions to improve operational efficiency and bidding performance.

## Responsibilities

- Conducted literature review
- Collected and analyzed quantitative data
- Collected and analyzed qualitative data
- Evaluated existing business processes
- Identified operational challenges
- Developed improvement recommendations

## Research Methods

- Literature Review
- Secondary Research
- Quantitative Analysis
- Qualitative Analysis
- Business Process Analysis

## Key Findings

- Identified bottlenecks affecting bidding performance
- Evaluated operational effectiveness
- Proposed practical recommendations to improve bidding participation activities

## Skills Demonstrated

- Research Methodology
- Data Collection
- Data Analysis
- Business Process Evaluation
- Critical Thinking
- Report Writing
- Evidence-Based Recommendations
