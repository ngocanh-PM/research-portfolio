# Graduation Thesis

## Title
Improving Bidding Participation Activities in Construction Consulting Enterprises

## Institution
National Economics University

## Research Objective
This research evaluates bidding participation activities in a construction consulting company and proposes solutions to improve operational efficiency and bidding performance.

## Research Methods
- Literature Review
- Secondary Data Analysis
- Business Process Evaluation
- Qualitative and Quantitative Analysis

## Key Contributions
- Identified bottlenecks in bidding processes
- Evaluated operational performance
- Proposed practical improvement recommendations

## Skills Demonstrated

- Data Analysis
- Business Process Analysis

- Evidence-Based Recommendations
